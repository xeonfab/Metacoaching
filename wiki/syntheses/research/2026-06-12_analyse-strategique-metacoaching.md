## Clinical Evidence

**ThéraSomnia** (Hôpital Gui de Chauliac study):
- 83% improvement in insomnia severity
- 82% improvement in sleep efficiency
- **91% reduction or cessation of hypnotic medication** ← headline stat

**LineCoaching** (real-world evidence, ~20,000 users):
- **79% maintained weight loss at 1–3 years** post-program
- Satisfaction: 4.8/5 on Avis Vérifiés (646 reviews since Dec 2015); 81% five-star ratings

## Business Models

### B2C — SaaS
Two-tier pricing: Autonomie (~25 €/month) and Coaching Premium (~45 €/month). Aggressive first-month promos (e.g. 4 € instead of 19 €). Auto-renewal after initial engagement period (typically 3 months), dropping to Autonomie rate without new synchronous sessions.

### B2B — QVT/Workplace
Angle: stress = #1 cause of sick leave (40% of employees affected), costs 12,600 €/year/employee. 77.5% of HR directors interested in health coaching. Turnkey integration with anonymized back-office reporting.

### B2B2C — Insurance ([Partenaires Assureurs](../entities/Partenaires_Assureurs.md))
Confirmed partners: AXA, Macif, Matmut (via Ociane), BPCE, MMA, GMF, MAAF.
Three value axes for insurers: (1) risk management / claims reduction, (2) differentiation & retention, (3) regulatory compliance ("haut degré de solidarité" contracts).

### ADELI Reimbursement Lever
Psychologists hold ADELI numbers registered with ARS → allows official invoicing → users claim reimbursement from complementary health insurance (Harmonie, Unéo, Malakoff Humanis, AG2R, Aésio…). See [ADELI & Remboursement](../concepts/ADELI_Remboursement.md).

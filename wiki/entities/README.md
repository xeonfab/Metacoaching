# 👥 Entities

> People, organisations, clients, banks, partners, squads. **One MD file per entity.**

This folder captures the *who* of your product work — everyone and every org that matters in your scope.

---

## What goes here

✅ **Yes**
- People you interact with (colleagues, managers, PMs, devs, designers, CS, Sales)
- Companies & competitors
- Clients & key client contacts
- Banks, partners, vendors
- Squads / teams (as entities in their own right)

❌ **No**
- One-off mentions (don't create a page for someone you met once)
- Abstract concepts → `wiki/concepts/`

> **Rule of thumb**: if the person/org will come up in 3+ future conversations, they deserve a page.

---

## Filename convention

`First_Last.md` for people (underscore separator, PascalCase).
`Org_Name.md` for orgs (underscore separator).

Examples: `Hind_Bidal.md`, `Societe_Generale.md`, `Thermocompact.md`.

---

## Schema

Copy [`_template.md`](_template.md) to start. Every entity page should have:

- **Front-matter** with `last_reviewed: YYYY-MM-DD`
- **One-line identity** (role, org)
- **Relevance** — why they matter to your work
- **Key topics** — what they own / drive / bring to the table
- **Interactions** — log of meetings, messages, key moments (append-only)
- **Sources** — raw files that mention them

---

## Philosophy

Entity pages are **living dossiers**. Every time you interact with someone, append to their Interactions log. Six months later, you have an audit trail of your working relationship.

Cross-link heavily — link people to the concepts they own and the syntheses they appear in.

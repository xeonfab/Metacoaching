# Pr Michel Billiard & Dr Yves Dauvilliers

**Role**: Scientific committee — [Méta-Coaching](Metacoaching.md)
**Domain**: Sleep pathology, neurology
**Institution**: Hôpital Gui de Chauliac, CHU Montpellier (Centre National de Référence des Troubles du Sommeil)
**MOC**: [MOC Méta-Coaching](../mocs/MOC_Metacoaching.md)

---

## Overview

Two leading sleep specialists involved in the clinical validation and scientific animation of **ThéraSomnia**. Pr Billiard is an internationally recognized neurologist; Dr Dauvilliers is an expert in sleep disorders. Both are affiliated with the national sleep reference center at CHU Montpellier.

---

## Key Contributions to Méta-Coaching

- Clinical evaluation and validation of **ThéraSomnia**
- Pr Billiard animates live webinars for ThéraSomnia users
- The CHU Montpellier study conducted under their institutional supervision produced the headline clinical results: 91% reduction in hypnotic medication use, 83% improvement in insomnia severity

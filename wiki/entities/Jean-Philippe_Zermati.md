# Dr Jean-Philippe Zermati

**Role**: Scientific committee — [Méta-Coaching](Metacoaching.md)
**Domain**: Nutritional medicine, anti-diet approach
**MOC**: [MOC Méta-Coaching](../mocs/MOC_Metacoaching.md)

---

## Overview

Recognized French nutritionist and medical doctor. Co-creator of [LineCoaching](Metacoaching.md) alongside [Dr Apfeldorfer](Gerard_Apfeldorfer.md). President of honor of the **GROS** (Groupe de Réflexion sur l'Obésité et le Surpoids). Their joint work has significantly influenced modern French dietary practice and the clinical rejection of caloric restriction diets.

---

## Key Contributions to Méta-Coaching

- Co-designed the clinical protocol for **LineCoaching**
- Theoretical anchor: the harm of external dietary rules (calorie counting, food categorization) in disconnecting individuals from physiological hunger signals
- Key concept: [cognitive restriction](../concepts/Restriction_Cognitive.md) as the root cause of yo-yo weight cycling

# Dr Gérard Apfeldorfer

**Role**: Scientific committee — [Méta-Coaching](Metacoaching.md)
**Domain**: Eating disorders, psychotherapy, psychiatry
**MOC**: [MOC Méta-Coaching](../mocs/MOC_Metacoaching.md)

---

## Overview

Eminent French psychotherapist and psychiatrist specializing in eating behavior disorders. Co-creator of [LineCoaching](Metacoaching.md) and ThéraSéréna. Works closely with [Dr Jean-Philippe Zermati](Jean-Philippe_Zermati.md) on the anti-diet / [cognitive restriction](../concepts/Restriction_Cognitive.md) approach. Together they are among the leading advocates of the non-restrictive eating methodology in France.

---

## Key Contributions to Méta-Coaching

- Co-designed the clinical protocol for **LineCoaching** (eating behavior / weight management)
- Co-designed **ThéraSéréna** (stress management)
- Theoretical foundation: deconstruction of cognitive restriction, rehabilitation of intuitive eating and mindful eating

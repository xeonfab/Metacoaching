## Business Model

- **B2C**: SaaS subscriptions — Autonomie (~25 €/mo) / Coaching Premium (~45 €/mo). First-month promos. Auto-renewal post-commitment period.
- **B2B**: Workplace health (QVT). ROI angle: stress costs 12,600 €/year/employee, affects 40% of workforce.
- **B2B2C**: Programs embedded in insurer offers. Partners: [AXA, Macif, GMF, MAAF, MMA, BPCE, Matmut](Partenaires_Assureurs.md).
- **Financing lever**: [ADELI reimbursement](../concepts/ADELI_Remboursement.md) via complementary health insurance.

---

## Clinical Evidence

- **ThéraSomnia**: 91% reduced/stopped hypnotics; 83% insomnia severity improvement (CHU Montpellier study)
- **LineCoaching**: 79% maintained weight loss at 1–3 years (20k users, real-world evidence); 4.8/5 satisfaction (646 reviews)
- Studies: CHRU Lille + Institut Pasteur de Lille (LineCoaching); Hôpital Gui de Chauliac, CHU Montpellier (ThéraSomnia)

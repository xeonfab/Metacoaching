## Overview

French psychiatrist specializing in stress modeling and psychophysiological regulation. Principal clinical architect of **ThéraSéréna**, Méta-Coaching's stress management program.

---

## Key Contributions to Méta-Coaching

- Designed the full clinical protocol for **ThéraSéréna** (7 phases, ~125 days total)
- Key therapeutic approaches used: deep relaxation techniques (diaphragmatic breathing, progressive muscle relaxation), cognitive restructuring of stress amplifiers (catastrophizing, overgeneralization), assertiveness training, time management

# Insurance & Mutual Partners

**Type**: B2B2C distribution partners
**MOC**: [MOC Méta-Coaching](../mocs/MOC_Metacoaching.md)

---

## Overview

Méta-Coaching has secured partnerships with major French insurance and mutual organizations, embedding its digital health programs into their member offer portfolios. This B2B2C channel is the primary strategic growth lever, enabling scale distribution without direct B2C acquisition costs.

## Value Proposition for Insurers

1. **Risk management**: prevent chronic diseases (diabetes, cardiovascular, depression) → reduce future claims
2. **Differentiation & retention**: high-value service in a commoditized market; white-label or co-branded portal option
3. **Regulatory compliance**: helps qualify collective insurance contracts as "haut degré de solidarité"

---

## Relevance to the Mission

Reporting to insurer partners is a high-priority automation target. Current state of reporting workflows (manual vs. automated) is to be assessed in the audit.

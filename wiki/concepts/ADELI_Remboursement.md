# ADELI Number & Insurance Reimbursement

**Context**: French health professional registration system + complementary health insurance reimbursement mechanism
**MOC**: [MOC Méta-Coaching](../mocs/MOC_Metacoaching.md)

---

## Definition

**ADELI** is the French national directory of health professionals, managed by the ARS (Agences Régionales de Santé). Any licensed psychologist practicing in France must be registered in ADELI to legally practice and to issue official healthcare invoices.

---

## Relevance to Méta-Coaching

Méta-Coaching employs psychologists who hold valid ADELI numbers registered with their respective ARS. This enables the company to issue **official healthcare invoices** (including the practitioner's ADELI number) for sessions conducted within the Coaching Premium subscription tier.

**Business model implication**: Users can submit these invoices to their complementary health insurance (mutuelle) for partial or full reimbursement under:
- "Médecine douce" annual allowances
- Psychology session reimbursement packages

**Known reimbursing organizations**: Harmonie Mutuelle, Unéo, Malakoff Humanis, AG2R, Aésio Mutuelle (and others).

Méta-Coaching provides a **pre-quote generation tool** so users can verify reimbursement eligibility with their insurer *before* subscribing — removing the last purchase barrier.

---

## Strategic Importance

This mechanism is a core **B2C conversion lever**: it reduces the net out-of-pocket cost for users and aligns Méta-Coaching with the broader French health financing ecosystem. It also reinforces the "medical device" positioning (notably for ThéraSomnia).

# Modèle Phygital (AI + Human Psychologist)

**Also known as**: Phygital model, hybrid delivery model
**MOC**: [MOC Méta-Coaching](../mocs/MOC_Metacoaching.md)

---

## Definition

"Phygital" (physical + digital) describes [Méta-Coaching](../entities/Metacoaching.md)'s delivery architecture: AI expert systems handle continuous, personalized program delivery, while licensed human psychologists provide oversight, synchronous sessions, and asynchronous messaging support.

The AI does not replace the human — it amplifies and scales the human's reach.

---

## How It Works

1. **AI layer** (always on):
   - Diagnostic intake via questionnaires → clinical profiling
   - Personalized path assignment and dynamic real-time adjustment
   - Module sequencing, reminders, progress tracking
   - Dropout risk detection (2025–2026 roadmap: predictive)
   - Triggers alerts to human psychologist when needed

2. **Human layer** (Coaching Premium tier):
   - Synchronous sessions: 1–3 phone/video calls of 30 min per commitment period
   - Asynchronous: unlimited messaging / chat support between sessions
   - Psychologists cited by users: Amandine, Mathilde (examples from reviews)
   - All psychologists hold [ADELI numbers](ADELI_Remboursement.md) registered with ARS

---

## Clinical Impact

User testimony and satisfaction data (LineCoaching: 4.8/5, 646 reviews) consistently attribute program adherence and outcomes to the human psychologist's availability and empathy — especially asynchronous support outside scheduled sessions. The human layer is the retention mechanism.

---

## Relevance to the Mission

Automation opportunities exist at the interface between the AI layer and back-office operations (alert routing, session scheduling, KPI reporting to insurers). Automation must not intrude into the clinical relationship or handle patient-level data outside [HDS](HDS.md) perimeter.

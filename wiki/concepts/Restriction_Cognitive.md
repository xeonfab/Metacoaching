# Restriction Cognitive (Cognitive Restriction)

**Domain**: Nutritional psychology, eating behavior
**Authors**: Drs [Apfeldorfer](../entities/Gerard_Apfeldorfer.md) & [Zermati](../entities/Jean-Philippe_Zermati.md)
**MOC**: [MOC Méta-Coaching](../mocs/MOC_Metacoaching.md)

---

## Definition

Cognitive restriction describes the psychological state induced by externally imposed dietary rules — calorie counting, food weighing, "good food / bad food" categorization. These rules disconnect the individual from their internal physiological signals (hunger, satiety, gustatory satisfaction).

**Mechanism of harm**: The imposed restriction creates unbearable frustration → loss of control → compulsive eating (hyperphagia) → guilt → renewed restriction → yo-yo weight cycling. The restriction itself perpetuates the disorder it was meant to solve.

---

## Thérapeutique Approach (LineCoaching)

The [LineCoaching](../entities/Metacoaching.md) program was built to **deconstruct** cognitive restriction by rehabilitating:
- **Intuitive eating**: reconnecting with physiological hunger and satiety signals
- **Mindful eating**: slowing down, maximizing sensory pleasure to reach satisfaction with smaller quantities
- **Emotional tolerance**: accepting emotional eating urges without guilt, preventing escalation into binge episodes

The 9-step protocol operationalizes this deconstruction over ~3 months: observation → hunger discovery → tasting → anti-perfectionism → gustatory satiation → full satiety → evening management → negative thought defusion → emotion management.

---

## Clinical Evidence

Real-world evidence on ~20,000 LineCoaching users: **79% maintained weight loss at 1–3 years** post-program — vs. >95% yo-yo recurrence rate with traditional caloric restriction diets within 5 years (scientific literature benchmark).

---

## Strategic Relevance

This concept is the theoretical foundation for Méta-Coaching's expansion into **type-2 diabetes prevention**: the same cognitive restriction deconstruction methodology, applied algorithmically to metabolic disease prevention, would bring Méta-Coaching into formal care pathways.

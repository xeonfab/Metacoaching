# TCC — Thérapies Cognitives et Comportementales

**Also known as**: CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy) in English; TCC-I when applied specifically to insomnia
**MOC**: [MOC Méta-Coaching](../mocs/MOC_Metacoaching.md)

---

## Definition

Evidence-based psychotherapy paradigm postulating that maladaptive or pathological behaviors (chronic insomnia, binge eating, anxiety responses, smoking) are maintained by dysfunctional thought patterns (cognitions) and conditioned emotional reactions shaped by the individual's history and environment.

TCC does not treat symptoms palliatively. It targets the structural modification of behaviors by deconstructing cognitive schemas, modifying conditioned responses, and building new adaptive patterns.

---

## Relevance to Méta-Coaching

TCC is the **sole scientific foundation** for all [Méta-Coaching](../entities/Metacoaching.md) programs. Every program applies TCC methodology to a specific lifestyle pathology:

| Program | TCC application |
|---|---|
| LineCoaching | Deconstruction of [cognitive restriction](Restriction_Cognitive.md); intuitive eating, mindful eating |
| ThéraSomnia | TCC-I: sleep restriction, stimulus control, cognitive restructuring of sleep anxiety |
| ThéraSéréna | Stress TCC: relaxation, cognitive restructuring, assertiveness |
| ThéraTabac | Craving desensitization, behavioral habit modification |

**Digital TCC (e-CBT)**: Meta-analyses confirm that digitally-delivered CBT produces clinical outcomes comparable to face-to-face psychotherapy — the scientific basis for Méta-Coaching's product validity.
